PMSF
====

* 10 mM Phenylmethylsulfonyl fluoride (PMSF)
* MW = 174.19 g/mol
* PMSF is not soluble in water, so have to use isopropyl alcohol or DMSO

**Materials**

* Centrifuge tube
* Isopropyl alcohol
* PMSF powder
* Syringe
* 0.22 um syringe filter

**Procedure**

#. Dissolve 17.4 mg (0.0174 g) of PMSF powder in 10 mL of isopropyl alcohol. 
#. Mix well, vortex if necessary. 
#. Filter with 0.22 um syringe filter. 

Storage: Label, store at 4 C. PMSF is sensitive and can degrade easily in water, recommended to prepare freshly before use. Do not store for long period of time in fridge.
