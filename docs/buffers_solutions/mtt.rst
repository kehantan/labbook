MTT reagent
====

* 5 mg/mL MTT (can adjust to other concentrations if desired)
* Schematics for preparing MTT solution `here <https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1IIiZSPVcTfXI2qt4P9eORFXnCTqsc18I3t5ufM8Zi7M/edit?usp=sharing>`_.

**Materials**

* MTT powder 
* PBS
* Centrifuge tube
* Syringe
* Syringe filter (0.22 um)
* Aluminium foil

**Procedure**

#. Weight 0.05 g (50 mg) of MTT powder and transfer into a centrifuge tube. 
#. Add 10 mL of PBS into the centrifuge tube.

    * Prepare in a biosafety cabinet under sterile environment to prevent contamination. 

#. Vortex the solution until MTT is completely dissolve. 

    * Dissolve as much MTT powder as possible. Usually the powder would not dissolve completely, leaving behind some small chunks of solids. This is ok, we will sonicate and filter it out 
    
#. Sonicate to break small clumps. 
#. Filter with syringe filter. 
#. Wrap the centrifuge tube with aluminium foil. 

    * MTT is sensitive to light 

Storage: Label (:code:`MTT, 5 mg/mL, name initials, date prepared`), store in -20 C.
