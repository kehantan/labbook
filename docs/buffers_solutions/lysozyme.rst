Lysozyme stock
====

* 10,000 ug/mL lysozyme 
* Final concentration of lysozyme for 30 mL of lysis buffer is 100 ug/mL

**Materials**

* Microcentrifuge tube
* Distilled H2O 
* Lysozyme powder 

**Procedure**

#. Weight 10 mg (0.01 g) of lysozyme powder into a microcentrifuge tube. 
#. Add 1 mL of distilled H2O into the microcentrifuge tube. 
#. Mix well, vortex if necessary. 

Storage: Can store at 4 C, but recommended prepare before use
