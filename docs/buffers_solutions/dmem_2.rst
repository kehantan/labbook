DMEM
=====

**Materials**

* Milli-Q/Ultra pure water (2 L)
* Autoclaved empty bottle (500 mL)
* Vacuum filter cup, 0.22 um pore size 
* DMEM powder
* Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3)
* Magnetic stirrer
* Vacumm pump
* pH meter 
* Concentrated HCl

**Procedure**

#. Add 2 L of Milli-Q/Ultra pure water into plastic bucket
#. Add 2 packs of DMEM powder into water
#. Stir with magnetic stirrer until DMEM powder completely dissolve in the water
#. Add 7.4 g of NaHCO3 into DMEM
#. Adjust pH of media to desired range (7.2-7.4) with HCl or NaOH using dropper
#. Filter DMEM into autoclaved empty bottle (450 mL/bottle) using cup filter inside a biosafety cabinet
#. Label
#. Seal the bottle with parafilm and store in 4 C
