Buffer B
====

* 100 mM Tris-HCl, 300 mM NaCl, 6 M urea
* It's not strictly necessary, but it is best to prepare 2 buffer B: one for bench use, another to pre-cooled in cold room. 

**Materials**

* Empty bottle 
* :ref:`1M tris-hcl <tris-hcl>`
* :ref:`5M NaCl <nacl>`
* distilled H2O 
* Magnetic stirrer
