PBS (1x)
====

**Materials**

* :ref:`20x PBS <pbs20x>`
* MilliQ/Ultra pure H2O
* Bottle
* Sero pipette

**Procedure**

#. Add 950 mL of MilliQ/Ultra pure H2O into 1 L empty bottle. 
#. Add 50 mL 20x PBS into 950 mL MilliQ water. Mixed well.

Storage: Label, seal with parafilm, and store at bench.
