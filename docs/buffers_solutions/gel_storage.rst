Gel-storage solution
====================

* Final concentration = Glacial acetic acid, 10% (v/v)

**Materials**

* Glacial acetic acid 
* Distilled H2O 

**Procedure**

#. Add 50 mL of acetic acid to 450 mL of distilled H2O.
#. Mix well. 
#. Label and store at bench.
