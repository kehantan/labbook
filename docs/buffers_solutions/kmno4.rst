KMnO4 stain
====

* For TLC stain

**Materials** 

* KMnO4 powder
* :ref:`10% NaOH <naoh_10pct>`
* K2CO3
* Dist H2O 

+------------+--------+---------+
| Component  | Weight | Vol     |
+============+========+=========+
| KMnO4      | 1.5 g  | --      |
+------------+--------+---------+
|K2CO3       | 10 g   | --      |
+------------+--------+---------+
|10% NaOH    | --     | 1.25 mL | 
+------------+--------+---------+
| Dist H2O   |        | 200 mL  |
+------------+--------+---------+

**Procedure** 

#. Dissolve KMnO4 and K2CO3 in dist H2O
#. Add NaOH

Storage: Room temp
