.. _amc:

AMC stock
====

* 10 mg/mL AMC (57,084.14202535 uM)
* MW = 175.18 g/mol

**Materials**

* Micropipette 
* Micropipette tips  
* Microcentrifuge tube
* Waste beaker 
* DMSO 
* Weighing balance 
* Spatula 

**Procedure**

#. Weight 10 mg (0.01 g) of AMC powder into a microcentrifuge tube. 
#. Add 1 mL of DMSO into the microcentrifuge tube. Mix well. 

Storage: -20 C
