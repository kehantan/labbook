Compound DMSO plate 
====

* Compound stock = 20 mM 
* Calculate amount of DMSO needed to make 20 mM stock solution, can uses online molarity calculator
* :math:`V = \frac{m}{C \times MW}` 

    * V = Volume (Litre) 
    * m = Compound mass (g)
    * C = Concentration (mol/L)
    * MW = Molecular weight (g/mol)

**Materials**

* 96 well clear plate, v-bottom
* DMSO 
* Eppendorf tube 
* Weighing balance
* Spatula 
* Multichannel pipette 
* Micropipette 

**Procedure** 

#. Weigh 5 mg of compound into eppendorf tube. 
#. Add calculated volume of DMSO required to make compound stock solution with concentration of 20 mM. 
#. Vortex. 
#. Add 100 uL of DMSO into well B2-G12 clear 96 well, v-bottom plate.
#. Add 200 uL of compound stock solution into B1-G1. 1 row for each compound. 
#. Transfer 100 uL from B1-G1 to B2-G2 with multichannel pipette. Mix well. 
#. Repeat the steps from B2-G2 to B10-G10. Last 2 columns reserve for virus control and cell only (vehicle control). 
#. Seal plate with parafilm and keep in -20 C. 
#. Remaining stock in eppendorf tube can be kept in -20 C. 

**Notes:**

* Compound weight can change according to how much of the compound is available, if only 2 mg is available then calculate the required volume of DMSO to add to 2 mg of compound to make 20 mM stock solution.
* Final volume of dilution can also be adjusted, can reduce to 50 uL or lower if not sufficient for 100 uL.
