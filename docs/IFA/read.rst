Read plate
====

* Perkin Elmer Operetta, Harmony software

#. Turn on UPS plug. Long press 'ON' button on UPS.  
#. Turn on light source by pressing power button on bottom. 
#. Press blue round button on the side of Operetta.
#. Trun on power plug of PC and turn on PC. 
#. Log in windows and Harmony software. 
#. Press 'Open Lid'. 
#. Lift Operetta lid. 
#. Remove 96 wp cover. Hold latch, place 96 wp, then release latch. 
#. Close Operetta lid.
