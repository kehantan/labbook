Gel-fixing solution
====

* 25% (v/v) isopropanol, 10% (v/v) glacial acetic acid

**Materials**

* Isopropanol 
* Glacial acetic acid 
* Distilled H2O  

**Procedure**

#. Add 25 mL of isopropanol and 10 mL of acetic acid into 35 mL of distilled H2O. 
#. Mix well.
#. Label and store at bench.
